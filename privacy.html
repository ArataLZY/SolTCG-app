Privacy policy

SolTCG is a marketplace for trading cards, run in Saudi Arabia. This page describes what the app records, where it is kept, and how to have it removed. It describes what the app does today.

When you create an account

Signing up records an email address, a password, and the username you choose. The email identifies your account and is where confirmation and password reset links are sent. The username is the public handle shown next to your listings and your messages.

Your profile answers

The first-run questions are optional and every answer stays editable from your profile. What you answer is stored with your account: your city, what you are here for, which printings you collect, and whether you have sold cards online before.

Your profile picture

If you set one, it is stored with your account and shown to other people: beside your name on your listings, and in your conversations. It is served from a public web address, so anyone who has that address can open the image, and it can be cached by the browser or by the network in between.

Choosing a new picture replaces the old one, and removing it takes it off your profile. You can change or remove it at any time from your profile.

Your listings

A listing records the catalog card you picked, your asking price in SAR, the condition you chose, the city, any notes you wrote, and three photos of the physical card. The three photos - front, back and a condition close-up - are required before a listing can be submitted for review.

Saving a card, and sending a request to buy a listing, are also recorded against your account.

Messages, blocks and reports

Direct messages between accounts are stored so both sides can read the thread. Blocking an account is recorded so the block holds on our side. A report is stored for staff to review.

Text you write in a listing, a message, a purchase request, a report, your username or your city is checked against a list of blocked words before it is saved.

Photos are screened on your device

Before a seller photo or a profile picture is uploaded, the app checks it for explicit content with a classifier that runs inside the app. That check uses no recognition service and the photo is not sent anywhere for it.

SolTCG takes no payment

SolTCG does not process payment and holds no money. Buyers and sellers arrange payment and handover between themselves, outside the app.

Where it is stored

Accounts, listings, messages, reports and the records above are stored in a Supabase project: a Postgres database, with seller proof photos in private object storage. A stored photo is opened through a short-lived link rather than a public file address.

Other companies involved

  • Supabase provides the database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Card catalog data comes from TCGdex, and the market reference price shown for a printing comes from TCGplayer pricing carried in that data.
  • Card artwork is loaded from third-party image hosts: PokemonTCG.io, Limitless TCG, and the official Japanese Pokemon card site, pokemon-card.com. Your device asks those hosts for the image, so they see that request.
  • The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so Google serves that font file to your device.

These providers also receive whatever their own services normally record, such as IP addresses.

How long it is kept

Nothing expires on a timer. An account keeps its data until the account is deleted, and a listing keeps its data until the listing is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app, open your profile, open Settings, and use Delete account. You type your username to confirm, and the deletion cannot be undone. The account and its personal data are removed, not just switched off.

Deleting your account removes your profile row and username, your sign-in, your stored profile picture, every listing you own whatever its status together with its proof photos and stored image files, the purchase requests made on your listings and any you made yourself, your saved cards, blocks in both directions, the conversations you took part in with their messages, and any staff roles.

One thing is kept

If you filed a report about somebody else's listing, or recorded a moderation note about somebody else's listing as staff, that record stays in the moderation history with your account identifier removed. It no longer points at you.

This is the only exception. It is kept because moderators still need the history of a listing that belongs to somebody else.
