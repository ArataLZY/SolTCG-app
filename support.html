How to reach us, how to report something, and how to delete your account.

Email is the way to reach us. Say what happened and, if it is about a listing, which listing. We do not promise a reply time.

Report a listing or a person

Reports are filed from a conversation in the app, and they go to the staff review queue. Reporting needs a signed-in account.

  1. Open the listing and tap Message to start a conversation with the seller.
  2. In the conversation, tap the flag at the top.
  3. Choose a reason, add anything that helps staff review it, and send the report.

Block someone

The same conversation header has a block button. A blocked account cannot message you. Tap the same button again to unblock.

Delete your account

  1. Open your profile and then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account, and type your username to confirm.

Deletion is permanent and removes your account and its personal data, including your listings and their photos and your messages. The privacy policy lists exactly what is removed and the one moderation record that is kept without your account attached to it.

If deleting inside the app fails, email us and say so.
